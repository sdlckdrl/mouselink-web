Mouse and keyboard sharing tools compared

Compare mouse and keyboard sharing tools for Windows, Mac and Android.

From a Windows PC to Android, Mac and other PCs

With OneMouse, move the cursor past the edge of your Windows screen to control an Android phone or tablet, another Windows PC, or a Mac on its own display. Mac requires Apple silicon and macOS 13 or later.

  • Same network: pair by QR code or pairing code without signing in
  • Nearby Windows → Android: Bluetooth without a router (beta)
  • Share copied text and files over supported connections

Sign in with the same Google Account to find and connect to your devices from the OneMouse device list on Windows, Mac, and Android. Whether they can connect depends on your network.

Before you connect

What’s the difference between direct control and mirroring?

With direct control, you look at the device’s own screen. With mirroring, you control it in a window on your computer.

Does OneMouse require a Google sign-in?

No Google sign-in is required to pair on the same network by QR or pairing code, or to connect compatible devices over Bluetooth.
