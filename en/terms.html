Terms of Use

These terms describe the basic conditions for using the OneMouse app and website across a Windows main PC, Android, another Windows PC, and a macOS 13+ secondary Mac.

Effective date: July 28, 2026

01

Service summary and roles

OneMouse connects a Windows 10/11 main PC to Android or another Windows PC for input control, file and folder transfer, clipboard features, and supported remote screen features. macOS 13+ secondary support is planned after signed distribution and real-Mac validation; Mac will not act as the main controller.

02

Connection requirements

The devices generally need to be on the same Wi-Fi or LAN and able to communicate directly. They may alternatively connect over Bluetooth after system pairing. Guest Wi-Fi, AP isolation, company security policy, firewalls, antivirus software, or denied Local Network permission on macOS 15+ may prevent connection.

03

User permissions and responsibility

Users are responsible for allowing the operating-system permissions needed for the features they choose. Android input requires Accessibility. After release, a secondary Mac requires Accessibility for input; Local Network is requested on macOS 15+ and Bluetooth access when Bluetooth is enabled. Screen & System Audio Recording is optional and used only for LAN-based Mac display sharing.

04

Free / Pro and platform scope

Android purchase or trial policies are handled inside the Android distribution. The Windows app does not process purchases, validate Android Pro status, or transfer Android entitlement between platforms. When released, the macOS secondary app is planned to require neither a login nor a separate paywall.

05

Android store billing

Google Play Billing is used for applicable Pro purchases and restoration in the Google Play Android app. The Galaxy Store build may be distributed as a paid app. The relevant store's terms and policies apply to those transactions.

06

Support scope

Current official support covers Windows 10/11 as the main controller plus Android and Windows secondary devices over Wi-Fi/LAN or Bluetooth. macOS 13+ secondary support is planned after the public build and real-Mac validation. Mac screen and window sharing is LAN-only. USB/ADB connection is not included in the user-facing support scope.

07

Limitations

OneMouse depends on network state, operating-system permissions, security software, hardware, and device manufacturer behavior. Continuous connection, input, screen sharing, or transfer cannot be guaranteed in every environment.
