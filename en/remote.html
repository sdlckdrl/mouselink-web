OneMouse remote screen demo

Try controlling a PC from a phone.

The monitor and phone in the image show the same game. Play with the phone’s on-screen gamepad.

Play mini-games with the phone

Pick a game on the PC screen. In the racer, use the stick to dodge cars, A to accelerate, and B to brake.

How this demo differs from the app

This browser game does not connect to real PC games or devices or access your screen. With OneMouse installed on supported Windows and Android devices, you can open a remote screen over the same Wi-Fi or a supported Google account connection.
