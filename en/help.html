I can't see my device

  1. Open OneMouse on both devices.
  2. For QR or code pairing, check that both devices use the same router. A wired PC is fine if it's connected to that router.
  3. For Google account connections, check that both devices (Windows, Mac or Android) are signed in to the same account and online. Then refresh the device list.
Mac, Bluetooth (beta) or public Wi-Fi

On Mac, check Local Network access if that option appears. For Bluetooth (beta), check that it is on and connected on both devices. Workplace or guest Wi-Fi may block communication between devices even on the same network.

My devices are connected but the cursor won't cross

  1. On Android, go to Settings → Accessibility → Installed apps and make sure OneMouse is enabled.
  2. Make sure the Windows device layout matches the physical positions on your desk.
  3. The entire screen edge isn't necessarily active. Move your cursor through the entry passage marked in the layout.
My Mac is connected but clicks don't work

Check the OneMouse Accessibility permission on your Mac. If you just granted it, quit and reopen the Mac app.

My connection keeps dropping

  1. If it drops only when the screen goes off, check Android's battery and power-saving settings.
  2. If it works at home but drops elsewhere, check whether the guest or workplace Wi-Fi blocks communication between devices.
  3. For Bluetooth (beta), first check the connection status on both devices.

Files, copying or screenshots don't work

  1. Check that both apps are connected and you've selected the correct receiving device.
  2. To send a file across screens, arrange your connected devices, drag the file across the edge and drop it on the destination screen.
  3. If you received a screenshot, open an app that supports pasting images and paste it there.
Receive folders and screenshot requirements

Transfer methods and device support depend on the app version and connection type. Pasting a screenshot as an image and saving it as a file are different operations.

The remote screen fails to open or shows black

  1. If the PC screen appears on your phone but you can't control it, check that View only is off.
  2. When opening the Android screen on your PC, accept the screen capture prompt on your phone.
  3. If your Mac screen looks black on the PC, check the Mac's Screen Recording permission.

I can't see phone notifications

  1. Check OneMouse's notification access in Android Settings.
  2. In OneMouse, check Send notifications to PC on the phone and Show device notifications on the PC.
  3. Make sure the list of apps allowed to send notifications includes the app you want.
